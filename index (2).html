Frostbuild Arena

Chop trees for wood, then build forts, ramps and towers. Switch to Edit to cut windows and doors into walls and floors.

Default controls (rebind in Settings): WASD move · Space jump · Shift sprint · Z wall · X floor (look up for a ceiling) · C ramp · V roof · G edit (hold click and drag over tiles, release or G to confirm, R reset) · Q or 1 pickaxe · M material · hold click to turbo build.
