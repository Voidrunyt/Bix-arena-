Frostbuild Arena

An open grass field, unlimited materials. Build forts, ramps and towers freely. Switch to Edit to cut windows and doors into walls and floors.

Default controls (rebind in Settings): WASD move · Space jump · Shift sprint · Z wall · X floor (look down to floor your own tile, forward to bridge, up for a level above) · C ramp (look down for stairs on your own tile) · V roof · G edit a build (click or drag over tiles to open/close instantly, G or Q to exit, scroll or R to reset the piece) · Q or 1 pickaxe · M material · hold click to turbo build.
